# ExpDate attribution

ExpDate was created at the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST) by Ahmet Cagatay Seker and Sang Chul Ahn.

A generalized framework for recognition of expiration dates on product packages using fully convolutional networks. Expert Systems with Applications 203 (2022), 117310. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2022.117310

Original source: https://felizang.github.io/expdate/index_expdate.html

Changes by RESERVE preparation: image bytes are unchanged; assets were addressed by SHA-256 and repackaged into shards. Source annotations were retained; task/split/quality flags, conservative normalized dates, grouping and audit manifests were added. No external label is presented as personally reviewed by the RESERVE user.

The original rights statement, including its third-party-rights clarification, is preserved in sources/ExpDate-original-README.md. The Hugging Face mirror's AFL-3.0 metadata differs from the original author's dataset license; source attribution and licensing here follow the author README and project dataset page.

The previous 982-image RESERVE corpus retains its own rights and label records inside original fields; CC BY 4.0 is not asserted over user-provided data.
